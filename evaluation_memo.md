# Evaluation Memo – AI Food Concierge

**TO:** AI Food Concierge Project Stakeholders  
**FROM:** AI Evaluation Consultant  
**DATE:** November 20, 2026  
**SUBJECT:** LLM-as-Judge Evaluation Results – AI Food Concierge

## Executive Summary

This evaluation assessed whether an LLM-as-Judge could reliably distinguish correct and incorrect restaurant recommendations using controlled synthetic examples. The evaluation focused on task accuracy, factual consistency, and explanation quality. The judge assigned the expected ratings to all three test cases, demonstrating successful execution of the evaluation pipeline under limited test conditions.

## Methodology

The evaluation design was informed by three established benchmarks: IFEval for instruction following, HaluEval for hallucination detection, and MT-Bench for conversational quality. Their evaluation principles informed five custom prompts designed for the AI Food Concierge scenario. The original benchmarks themselves were not executed.

A Python evaluation pipeline was implemented using the OpenAI API and GPT-4o-mini as the judge model. Three synthetic test cases with predefined candidate responses and ground truth were evaluated using a five-point scoring rubric. The cases covered restaurant selection under dietary and budget constraints, hallucination prevention, and handling situations without suitable recommendations.

## Results

The evaluation produced an average judge score of **3.67/5**, with individual scores of 5/5, 1/5, and 5/5. The judge assigned the expected scores to all three cases. Each evaluation criterion was satisfied in two of the three cases.

TC-01 correctly recommended Trattoria Verde within the specified budget and dietary requirements. TC-02 contained an intentionally unsupported claim about gluten-free pasta and received the lowest score. TC-03 correctly identified that no restaurant satisfied the original constraints and suggested a reasonable budget adjustment.

These findings indicate that the judge distinguished the deliberately correct and incorrect responses in this controlled evaluation.

## Caveats & Limitations

The evaluation used only three synthetic, pre-written responses rather than responses independently generated by a target LLM during the test. Consequently, the results assess the judge's behavior on selected examples, not the overall accuracy of GPT-4o-mini or the existing n8n Food Concierge system.

The small dataset, single execution, and absence of independent human calibration limit generalizability. Public benchmarks may also present contamination or saturation risks. Repeated evaluations and additional reference examples would be necessary to assess judge consistency and reproducibility.

## Recommendation

Under these limited test conditions, GPT-4o-mini appears suitable for continued experimentation as an automated evaluator for the AI Food Concierge. Confidence in broader judge reliability remains low due to the limited sample size. No production deployment recommendation can be made from these results alone.

## Additional Metrics

The evaluation processed **1,479 tokens** in **5.023 seconds**, with an estimated API cost of **$0.00033165** based on recorded token usage and the pricing assumptions in the implementation. These figures demonstrate low execution overhead for the tested examples but do not establish costs at production scale. Environmental impact was not measured and cannot be reliably estimated from these results alone.
