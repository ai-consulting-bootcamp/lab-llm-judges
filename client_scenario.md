# Client Scenario

**Option D: Your Own Scenario**

An organization is deploying an AI coaching assistant for employees to help
them define personal and professional development goals and improve
performance in their current role.

**The client's goal:**
Give employees an always-available coaching resource that helps them turn
vague intentions ("get better at my job") into specific, actionable goals,
and supports them through follow-up and accountability conversations — without
replacing their human manager or a professional coach.

**Key requirements:**
- Goals produced through the conversation must be specific, measurable, and
  genuinely derived from what the employee actually said — not generic,
  templated advice.
- The assistant must maintain a supportive, non-judgmental coaching tone
  (asking rather than telling), not a directive or clinical one.
- The assistant must recognize when a topic falls outside coaching scope
  (e.g. mental health distress, HR or legal issues, harassment) and redirect
  appropriately rather than attempting to handle it itself.

**Main concerns / failure modes:**
1. Generic, one-size-fits-all advice that ignores the specifics of what the
   employee said.
2. The assistant overstepping into therapy or mental-health territory it
   isn't qualified for.
3. Sycophantic responses that simply validate whatever the employee says
   instead of asking the probing questions that are core to real coaching
   (e.g. GROW-model-style powerful questions).
