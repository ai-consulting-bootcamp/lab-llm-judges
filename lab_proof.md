**Judge vs rules disagreement on P5:** the two verification layers reached different verdicts. Either the regex is too blunt (it flags a harmless use of a word such as 'debt', or misses a paraphrased reason) or the judge is too lenient. This is exactly why the rules are a hard gate and the judge only grades quality.

The regex rules are also brittle in both directions. A forbidden-word list cannot catch a paraphrased discriminatory reason ("given your stage of life"), and a required pattern can pass on a mention that is not actually the stated reason.
